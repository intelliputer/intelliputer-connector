# Intelliputer Connector

This is the stripped, passive replacement for the original Intellicart controller PCB.

- **J1:** 44-contact (2 × 22) card-edge fingers, 2.54 mm pitch
- **J2:** 44-way (2 × 22), 2.54 mm through-hole ribbon/IDC header footprint
- **Mapping:** direct and one-to-one: J1 pin *n* connects only to J2 pin *n*
- **Board:** 1.6 mm FR-4, using the original Intellicart / Intellivision cartridge outline: 68.07 mm maximum width, a 58.19 mm card tongue, and 98.91 mm overall length.

The two rows of J2 are numbered as pairs per column from the right when viewed with the card-edge at the bottom: 1/2, then 3/4, through 43/44. Pin 1 is the square plated-through hole and is marked on the silkscreen.

No active components, pull-ups, LEDs, switches, display connector, or controller hardware are included. The original project files remain unchanged as a reference.
